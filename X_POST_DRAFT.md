## Main post

Rogue AI agents aren't science fiction anymore. They're running now, and in swarms: dozens of agents handing work to each other, spawning more agents, with real access to shells, files, money and the internet. One goes off the rails and the rest follow, faster than any human can watch.

Most setups have nothing standing between those agents and real damage.

I'm not selling anything. agent-warden is free, open-source Rust built for one reason: prevention. It blocks every action you didn't allow, logs everything, and shuts a rogue agent and everything it spawned down on the spot.

If you're accountable for your agents, you deserve the power to protect yourself.

It's a small start. But every big safeguard started small, and I'd rather act now than watch.

We can't wait for someone else to fix this. Help build it:
